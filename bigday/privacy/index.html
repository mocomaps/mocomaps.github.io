Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Big Day does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • Your days, notes and photos are stored on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud database. They are synced by Apple's iCloud service between your devices. The developer has no access to them.
  • Photos you add are chosen by you through the system photo picker. Big Day only sees the photos you pick. If you choose “Save to Photos”, Big Day asks for permission to add that photo to your library; it never reads your library.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to any server.
  • Big Day contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Deleting the app removes its on-device data. To remove synced data, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Big Day.

中文

隐私政策

最后更新：2026年10月25日

“大日子”不收集、不存储、也不分享任何个人信息。

  • 你的日子、笔记和照片保存在你的设备上；如果你登录了 iCloud，也会保存在你自己的私人 iCloud 数据库中，由 Apple 的 iCloud 服务在你的设备之间同步。开发者无法访问这些内容。
  • 照片由你通过系统照片选择器挑选，“大日子”只能看到你选中的照片。当你选择“存储到照片”时，“大日子”会请求将该照片添加到你的图库的权限；它从不读取你的图库。
  • 通知在你的设备上本地安排，不会向任何服务器发送数据。
  • “大日子”不含任何分析、广告或追踪，也不使用第三方 SDK。

删除 App 会移除设备上的数据。若要删除已同步的数据，请前往 设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud › 管理储存空间 › 大日子。

如有疑问：zhaoyongniu@gmail.com
