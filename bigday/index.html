Support

Big Day counts down to birthdays, anniversaries and holidays on the Gregorian or Chinese lunar calendar, and keeps a photo album for every day once it has passed.

Need help? Email zhaoyongniu@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I add a lunar birthday?

Tap +, choose Lunar, and tap the date to pick it on the lunar calendar. Leave Repeats every year on and Big Day finds the right Gregorian date every year.

I'm not getting reminders

Open Big Day's Settings (the gear on Home) and check that notifications are on. If they're off, go to the iPhone Settings app › Notifications › Big Day and switch on Allow Notifications. You can choose what time reminders arrive in Big Day's Settings.

How do I add photos to a past day?

Open the History tab, tap the day, then tap Add Photos. Tap a photo to add a caption, or hold it to save it to your phone.

My days aren't showing on my other iPhone

Big Day syncs through your own iCloud. Make sure both phones are signed in to the same Apple Account and iCloud is turned on for Big Day (Settings › [your name] › iCloud).

How do I switch to Chinese?

Open Settings in Big Day and choose 中文 under Language.

怎样添加农历生日？

点按 +，选择农历，再点按日期，在农历日历上选好那一天。保持每年重复开启，“大日子”每年都会自动换算成对应的公历日期。

收不到提醒怎么办？

在首页点齿轮打开“设置”，确认通知已开启。如果已关闭，请前往 iPhone“设置” › 通知 › 大日子，开启“允许通知”。提醒时间可以在“大日子”的设置里选择。

怎样给过去的日子添加照片？

打开回忆，点按那一天，再点添加照片。点按照片可以写说明，长按可以存储到手机。

另一部 iPhone 上看不到我的日子

“大日子”通过你自己的 iCloud 同步。请确认两部手机登录的是同一个 Apple 账户，并为“大日子”开启了 iCloud（设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud）。
